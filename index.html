04 — Lab Notes

Building a robot that notices things.

At HTP Lab in Kathmandu — guided by Muni Sakya, Nepal's first computer manufacturer — a small team and I are assembling an InMoov upper-body humanoid from 3D-printed parts, servos, and stubbornness.

My side of the project is the mind: a Python master control system that fuses dual-camera vision with a vision-language model, Whisper for speech, an LLM reasoning layer with fallback chains, and Kokoro TTS for voice. DeepFace handles face and emotion recognition, while magnetic encoders and accelerometers give the robot proprioception — knowing where its own joints are.

The unglamorous work matters most: calibrating encoder values by hand, computing tyre revolution ratios for wheel odometry, tuning until the handshake feels like one.
